BMT Studio

BMT Studio, BMT'nin kendi videolarını kendi YouTube, Instagram ve TikTok hesaplarında yayımlamak için kullandığı kişisel bir masaüstü yayın aracıdır. Hazırlanan her video önce ekip tarafından izlenip onaylanır; araç yalnız onaylanan videoyu, bağlı hesaba, başlığı ve açıklamasıyla birlikte yükler.

BMT Studio herkese açık bir hizmet değildir; başka kişilerin hesaplarına bağlanmaz ve dışarıdan kullanıcı kabul etmez. Hangi bilgileri nasıl kullandığı Gizlilik Politikası'nda, kullanım koşulları Kullanım Şartları'nda yazılıdır.

English

BMT (Science • History • Technology) publishes one-minute documentaries. Each video opens with a single question, moves through real archive footage and states its sources openly.

Channels

  • Tarih (History) — the overlooked footnotes of big events, told with photographs, films and sound recordings of the time.
  • Bilim ve Teknoloji (Science and Technology) — from the inside of the Sun to the computer that flew to the Moon; documentary graphics drawn over NASA and ESA imagery.
  • İlginç Bilgiler (Curious Facts) — the stranger side of the world; one striking fact and its story in every video.

Every video

At most 90 seconds, vertical 1080×1920. Footage is public domain or freely licensed (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA); no AI-generated fake photographs. Every claim rests on at least two reliable sources, listed with the image licences in the video description.

BMT Studio

BMT Studio is the private desktop publishing tool BMT uses to post its own videos to its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. Every video is watched and approved by the team first; the tool then uploads only that approved video, with its title and description, to the connected account. BMT Studio is not a public service: it does not connect to anyone else's account and does not accept outside users. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
